Legal

Last updated 1 October 2026.

Your data, handled with care

What we collect

Only what we need to serve you.

Information we hold

When you enquire, book a test drive or service, or create an account, we collect your name, mobile number, email, vehicle registration number and pincode.

In this demo website, account details stay in your own browser’s storage and are not sent to any server.

  • Contact details you type into forms
  • Vehicle details for service bookings
  • Saved models and comparison choices (stored in your browser)
  • Service history linked to your registration number
How we use it

Purpose, not profiling.

Why we use it

We use your details to respond to enquiries, schedule services, process finance applications you request, and meet legal obligations such as RTO registration.

  • To call or message you about your enquiry
  • To send service reminders you opted into
  • To prepare quotes and invoices
  • To comply with the Motor Vehicles Act and tax rules
Sharing

Never sold, rarely shared.

Who we share with

We share data only with partners needed to complete what you asked for.

  • Partner lenders, if you apply for a loan
  • Insurers, when you buy or renew a policy
  • The manufacturer, for warranty and recall records
  • Government authorities where the law requires it
Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

You stay in control

You can ask to see, correct or erase your personal data, and withdraw consent at any time.

  • Request a copy of your data
  • Correct anything inaccurate
  • Ask us to delete your account
  • Withdraw marketing consent with one message
Security & retention

How long, and how safely.

Kept safe, kept briefly

We keep service records for 8 years for warranty and tax purposes and delete enquiry data after 24 months of inactivity.

  • Access limited to trained staff
  • Encrypted backups
  • Annual security review
  • Breach notification within 72 hours
