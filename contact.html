Before you visit.

Visiting us

Is there parking?

Yes — 40 covered visitor spaces and two EV fast chargers you can use free while you are with us.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome. For a specific variant or a home test drive, book a day ahead.

Can you call me back in Tamil?

Of course. Mention it in your message — we also speak English, Hindi and Telugu.

How do I reach you from the bus stand?

The showroom is 2.5 km from Salem New Bus Stand towards Omalur; most autos know “Stackly showroom”.
