About servicing with us.

Service, explained

Do you service cars bought elsewhere?

Yes. We service all petrol, diesel, hybrid and EV models from the brands we retail, wherever you bought the car.

Will servicing here affect my warranty?

No. We are an authorised service point; every job is logged to your car’s warranty record.

How do I pay?

UPI, cards, net banking or cash at the desk. Corporate fleets can be invoiced monthly.

Is pickup really free?

Within 15 km of the service centre, yes. Beyond that it is ₹15 per km each way.
