Line by line

What each part of the price means.

No mystery in the maths

Ex-showroom

The manufacturer’s price including GST and cess, before registration.

RTO / road tax

Tamil Nadu charges about 12% below ₹10 lakh and 15% above; EVs are currently exempt.

Insurance

1-year own damage plus 3-year third-party, as mandated for new cars.

TCS

1% tax collected at source on cars above ₹10 lakh — claimable in your income-tax return.

Accessories

Optional packs; you can skip them entirely and still get delivery.

EMI

Calculated with the standard reducing-balance formula on the loan amount.

Questions

About price and finance.

Money matters

Why must the down payment be 10–90%?

Lenders need at least 10% from you, and a loan below 10% of the price is usually not worth the processing fee.

Can I prepay the loan?

Most partner lenders allow part-prepayment after 6 EMIs; floating-rate loans have no foreclosure charge.

Is TCS an extra cost?

It is collected upfront but you can claim it back as tax credit when filing your return.

Will this price change?

Prices can change with manufacturer revisions or tax changes. An advisor can lock your quote for 7 days.
