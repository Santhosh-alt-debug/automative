Good to know before you come in

Can I book a test drive at home?

Yes. Within 25 km of our Salem showroom we bring the car to you at no charge; across Tamil Nadu we arrange it through partner outlets.

Do you take my old car in exchange?

We do. A certified evaluator inspects it in 30 minutes and gives a written offer valid for 7 days, adjustable against any new or pre-owned car.

How long does delivery take?

Most variants in stock deliver within 7 days of finance approval. Specific colours or top variants can take 3–6 weeks; we tell you the date before you pay.

Are pre-owned cars covered by warranty?

Every certified pre-owned car gets a 1-year / 20,000 km warranty, a 150-point inspection report and free first service.

Your next drive starts with a coffee

Book a test drive and we will have the car warmed up, the AC cold and the filter coffee ready.
