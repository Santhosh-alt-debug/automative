Our story

From a corner showroom to Tamil Nadu’s favourite.

Built on word of mouth

Stackly Automotive began in 2012 when founder R. Venkatesan converted his family’s textile godown into a two-bay workshop. Customers came for honest estimates and stayed for the filter coffee.

Today we run a 40,000 sq ft showroom, a 14-bay service centre with three EV-certified bays, and branches in Coimbatore and Chennai — still owned by the same family.

What has not changed

  • The estimate you approve is the bill you pay
  • Every car is test-driven by a senior technician before return
  • A real person answers the phone, seven days a week
  • We publish our service prices online
